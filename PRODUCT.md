# Product

> Impeccable design-context adapter for the primary `apps/web` experience.
> Product truth remains in `AGENTS.md`, accepted ADRs, active specs, and the
> authored content pipeline.

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Adult players experiencing a short interactive cinema / visual-novel-like game
that combines authored Ink with bounded AI branches and player-selected character identity.

## Product Purpose

Deliver a playable, portable 20–30 minute experience whose comedy, cinematic
staging, choices, and controlled AI endings feel like one authored product.

## Positioning

SupaLuv is an adult black-humor sex comedy about robots, desire, and awkward
intimacy—not a romance app, porn generator, or generic AI companion.

## Brand Personality

Provocative, cinematic, mischievous.

## Anti-references

Sweet rom-com framing, free-form erotic chat, explicit pornography, card-game
UI, generic chatbot layouts, and visual spectacle that hides authored choices.

## Design Principles

- Keep authored story, current choice, and cinematic subject in clear hierarchy.
- Let still-first staging and restrained motion carry tone without blocking play.
- Make AI branches visibly bounded and return players to authored structure.
- Preserve content portability and avoid locking product truth into one engine.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide readable subtitles and dialogue, visible focus, keyboard and touch input,
reduced-motion behavior, content warnings where needed, and no information conveyed
only through sound or color.
