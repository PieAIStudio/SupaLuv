# ADR-0004: Black humor sex comedy — not romance

## Context

External reviews and some earlier copy framed SupaLuv as an “adult sci-fi
disaster **rom-com** / 黑色**爱情**喜剧.” That pulls agents and drafts toward
sweet pairing, healing romance, and “believe in love again” beats.

The owner rejects that frame. The product is **not** selling love belief or
a romantic-comedy arc. Young-audience energy, including sex-adjacent humor
that is **not dirty**, is intentional and required.

## Decision

### External pitch (what we claim)

- **Primary genre:** adult **black humor comedy** (黑色幽默喜剧).
- **Also accurate:** **sex comedy** (性喜剧) — desire, awkward bodies/status,
  transactional intimacy, shame, robots/AI ethics as joke material.
- **Not primary:** romance, rom-com, “黑色爱情喜剧,” sweet love story,
  destiny pairing, healing couple fantasy.

IP title **超级爱人** may stay as a brand/IP name; it does **not** mean the
story must be a romance product.

### In-story content (what the novel may do)

- Author **will** put in things young players enjoy that are **sex-related**,
  delivered as humor and social deconstruction — **not dirty**, not pornographic
  spectacle as the product purpose.
- Goal emotional register while playing: **excited, amused, a bit unhinged /
  high-energy joy** — not soft-focus romantic melting.
- Platform rules still apply (no free-form porn generator, no explicit nude
  gen as a goal, no non-consensual material). “Not dirty + humorous framing”
  is the house style that keeps platform risk manageable without flattening
  into PG romance.

### Hard no for agents (including future novel → Ink work)

When reading, summarizing, adapting, or expanding the novel:

1. Do **not** reframe the work as a love story or rom-com.
2. Do **not** “fix” scenes by making them sweeter, more sincere-romance,
   or more “they fall in love properly.”
3. Prefer embarrassment, status games, body/desire jokes, transactional
   absurdity, AI/robot intimacy ethics, and black humor punchlines.
4. Sex-adjacent material stays **comedy first**; never drift into porn-studio
   or AI sex-companion product behavior (still forbidden by AGENTS.md).

### One-line positioning (use this)

> 成人科幻 **黑色幽默 / 性喜剧**（不脏、好笑、会心跳加速）——**不是**让人重新相信爱情的甜宠浪漫。

English:

> Adult sci-fi **black humor / sex comedy** (not dirty, funny, high-energy) —
> **not** a sweet romance that sells “believe in love again.”

## Consequences

- Marketing, boot/title tags, store copy, and AI story adapters must follow
  this ADR over any archived “rom-com” language (including the 2026-07
  commercial audit archive).
- Content safety policy remains **adult-comedy**, not “romance with mild spice.”
- If a draft sounds like ordinary romantic VN, rewrite the tone before shipping.
